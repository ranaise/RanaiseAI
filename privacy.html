Privacy notice.

Last updated: October 2026. This notice describes the current early-stage Ranaise website and demonstration experience.

Information you choose to provide

If you request a demo, the form asks for your name, work email, organization, role, industry, team size, preferred timing, and optional WhatsApp number, revenue range, tools, and notes. We use these details to review and respond to your inquiry. Do not submit confidential customer, employee, or financial records in the request notes.

How requests are sent

When email delivery is configured, request details are sent through Resend to the Ranaise contact inbox at founder@ranaise.site. The submitted email address is used as the reply address. Cloudflare Turnstile processes technical signals to help prevent automated submissions; its verification token is checked by our server and is not included in the notification email. If automatic delivery is unavailable, your details remain in the form and the website may offer a prepared email link. We show a success confirmation only after the email provider accepts the request.

Technical demo data

The guided browser demo analyzes sample data or CSV files locally. Uploaded CSV contents are not automatically transmitted to our servers. The optional live Claude endpoint is unavailable unless configured separately. If activated, it sends your question and aggregate statistics to the AI provider, not the original CSV.

Infrastructure and records

The website is hosted on Vercel, which may process standard request metadata for service delivery and security. Resend processes demo inquiry details to deliver notification email; Cloudflare processes security-check signals. Emails and inquiries may be retained in our contact inbox as needed to handle correspondence. We do not claim to operate a production enterprise workspace or established customer-data platform.

Your choices

Providing optional details is voluntary. You can request correction or deletion of your inquiry, or ask us not to contact you again, by emailing founder@ranaise.site. Certain records may be kept when required for security or legal compliance.

Changes

We may update this notice as the product and its integrations evolve. The latest text will appear on this page.
