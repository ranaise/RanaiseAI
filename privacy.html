Privacy Notice

Last updated October 2026

Information we collect

When you request a demo, we collect the contact details and business information you choose to provide, such as your name, work email, organization, role and areas of interest. Some fields are optional.

How we use information

We use your details to respond to inquiries, arrange conversations and operate our website. We work with service providers for hosting, form security and email delivery. They may process information needed to provide those services.

Interactive Beta

CSV files uploaded to the interactive workspace are processed in your browser and are not automatically uploaded to our servers. If you choose to use an optional AI service when available, your question and a summary of the data may be shared with the AI provider.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete information you submitted, or request that we stop contacting you, by emailing founder@ranaise.site. We keep inquiries for as long as reasonably needed to handle them and meet applicable obligations.

We may update this notice as our services evolve.
